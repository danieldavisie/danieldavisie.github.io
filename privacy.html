Privacy policy

Donna is used only by invited users who sign in with their own Google account.

It accesses Gmail (read-only), Google Calendar and Google Tasks solely to answer the user's requests and send them digests. Email content is processed by a third-party AI model provider (via OpenRouter) to generate responses. Data is not sold, shared or used for advertising.

Access tokens are stored on a private server. Users can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
